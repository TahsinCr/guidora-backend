# Moderation lexicon attribution

The English (`en.txt`) and Turkish (`tr.txt`) lexicons are derived from
[LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words),
upstream revision `5faf2ba42d7b1c0977169ec3611df25a3c08eb13`.

The upstream project identifies Shutterstock, Inc. as the author and licenses
the lists under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0). The upstream lists are included as-is; Guidora's matching and
normalization code is separate project code. The lexicon is a heuristic
baseline, not a language authority or a substitute for human moderation.
